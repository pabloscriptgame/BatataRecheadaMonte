Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Fortune Tiger is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

We collect information you provide during registration (name, email, date of birth, address), payment information, and usage data on our platform.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide our services, verify your identity (KYC), process payments, prevent fraud, and communicate with you about offers and updates.

All data is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology. We never sell your personal data to third parties. Access to your data is strictly controlled.

We use cookies to enhance your experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect functionality.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact our support team to make such requests.
