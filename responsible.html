Responsible Gaming

Fortune Tiger is committed to providing a safe, fair, and responsible gambling environment. We believe gambling should always be fun — never a problem.

Our Tools

💰 Deposit Limits

Set daily, weekly, or monthly deposit limits to stay in control of your spending.

⏸️ Session Limits

Limit the time you spend on our platform per session.

❄️ Cool-Off Period

Take a break for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days without closing your account.

🚫 Self-Exclusion

Permanently or temporarily exclude yourself from our platform.

Signs of Problem Gambling

  • Spending more money or time gambling than you can afford
  • Gambling to escape problems or relieve stress
  • Lying to friends or family about gambling
  • Feeling unable to stop gambling
  • Neglecting work, family, or personal responsibilities

Get Help

If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, please reach out to a support organization:

  • GamCare: www.gamcare.org.uk
  • Gamblers Anonymous: www.gamblersanonymous.org
  • National Problem Gambling Helpline: 1-800-522-4700 (US)

You can also contact our support team and we will help you activate responsible gaming tools immediately.
